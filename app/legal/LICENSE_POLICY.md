Figure Tool's first-party source uses **Apache-2.0**. The full terms are in
[LICENSE](LICENSE), with attribution in [NOTICE](NOTICE). This covers the
original application code, documentation,
configuration, and build/test tooling in this repository. Existing notices in
third-party or contributed material remain applicable.

Original scientific illustrations, motifs, previews, and scene recipes use
**CC0-1.0**, separately from the code license. See the
[asset dedication](assets/LICENSE-ORIGINAL-ASSETS-CC0-1.0.md) and individual
source records. Reuse permission and inclusion in a reviewed product release
are separate decisions; an unfinished asset can have CC0 permission while its
product release review remains pending.

The 80 original drawings added on 2026-10-15 in Cell Structures & Stress,
Tumor Microenvironment, and Analysis Graphics apply that same original-artwork
CC0 default prospectively. Their finite scope and current repository-policy
basis are recorded in the [contribution notice](assets/LICENSE-CELL-STRESS-TME-ANALYSIS-CC0-1.0.md)
and the three pack-scoped records. They do not inherit or extend the historical
owner attestations dated 2026-10-10. Scientific and visual acceptance is recorded
separately from native editor qualification and the product-release decision.

Bundled fonts and libraries keep their original licenses. Full terms and
attributions are indexed in [vendor/NOTICE](vendor/NOTICE). Apache-2.0 does not
replace those terms. The application license does not impose conditions on a
user's own figures or data merely because they were created in Figure Tool.

This repository does not license the surrounding private Superbench application.
The native Figure Tool host has a separately scoped license in its owning
repository. Historical source snapshots and previously built installers are
retained unchanged; license files accompany newly prepared candidates.

The matching copies under `app/legal/` travel with ordinary tool packages whose
payload is confined to the editor directories. Update these copies with their
root originals; the package producer rejects missing or different terms when
`package.json` declares Apache-2.0. The original-artwork scope notice and full
CC0 text also travel under `app/legal/assets/` and must match their originals.
