## Scientific scope

- The microfluidic junction illustrates aqueous cell partitioning into droplets. It does not guarantee single-cell occupancy or a particular capture yield.
- The capture bead distinguishes a shared bead barcode, variable molecular identifier regions, and poly(dT) capture tails. Segment shapes are not actual nucleotide sequences; the tether count is illustrative.
- The spatial array depicts a tissue section associated with spatially barcoded capture positions. A capture position can contain RNA from multiple cells; the schematic makes no single-cell resolution claim.
- The in-situ RNA puncta field depicts RNA-probe-associated localization in cellular context. It is not acquired microscopy or measured data. Its dots do not encode molecule counts, expression levels, a named target, or a physical scale.

## Method references

- [Raj et al., Nature Methods (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.1253): RNA fluorescence in situ hybridization and punctate localization.
