# Cell architecture and transport: biological forms and clearer drawings

The family contains 28 editable drawings representing 20 concepts: all 16 prior drawings have been refreshed, eight additional views or states share existing concepts, and four distinct transport mechanisms have been added. The choices differ in biological arrangement or state, rather than palette or line weight.

The prior catalog, 16 SVGs, CC0 notice and October 10 rights record are preserved in `assets/scientific-packs/cell-architecture-transport/revisions/2026-10-13-family-expansion-predecessor`. Existing IDs, scientific names, region IDs, paint channels and default colors are retained. Previously saved figures keep their embedded artwork; editor save/open qualification is recorded separately by the integration runner. The original owner CC0 grant is preserved, and product-release approval remains a separate pending decision.

## Biological choices

| Concept | Existing drawing refreshed | Additional choice |
|---|---|---|
| Tight junction | Membrane cross-section with strand contacts | Intersecting strands in the membrane plane |
| Gap junction | Docked hemichannels with a continuous lumen | Modest en-face channel plaque |
| Focal adhesion | Actin–adhesion–membrane–matrix relationship | Elongated adhesion footprint and actin in the membrane plane |
| COPII transport | ER-connected bud | Detached coated carrier |
| COPI transport | Detached coated vesicle | Golgi-connected bud |
| Endosomal recycling | One connected sorting tubule | Several connected tubules |
| ER–mitochondrion contact | Sheet ER beside a mitochondrion | Tubular ER beside a separate mitochondrion |
| Autophagosome–lysosome interaction | Early post-fusion compartment retaining the inner autophagic membrane | Two separate sealed compartments before fusion |

The four new concepts are myosin-Va transport on actin, a post-fusion cis-SNARE bundle, an open-neck ESCRT-associated inward bud, and a CRM1/exportin–RanGTP–cargo nuclear-export complex.

All drawings are illustrative. They do not establish measured dimensions, molecular counts, membrane permeability, transport speed, force, reaction rates or sample-specific activity. Named regions remain editable; the three artwork treatments change presentation while retaining the vector geometry and biological parts.

Search aliases name the complete depicted entity or view, such as a docked gap-junction channel or a COPI-coated Golgi bud. Component terms—including claudin, cadherin, keratin, talin, Arf1 and intraluminal vesicle—remain contextual search tags. This keeps discovery useful without identifying a junction as one of its proteins or an endosome as one of its internal vesicles.

## Reproduction and review

`scripts/seed-cell-architecture-transport-assets.py` regenerates the current native SVGs and source metadata from the scientific inventory and original coordinate renderer. It preserves the predecessor and never writes a rights grant or an audit. `scripts/cell_architecture_family_renderers.py` contains the current original geometry; the earlier contact/transport renderers remain available as historical construction code.

`scripts/render-cell-architecture-family-review.py` renders all 28 drawings at actual 64, 256 and 512 pixels in Soft color, Technical ink and Cut paper using the application transformations. Its 84 full-resolution originals are for scientific and visual inspection; native insertion, part editing, palettes, Save and cold Open are separately qualified by the shared browser runner. Contact sheets are navigation aids only.

Author review found and corrected crowded intraluminal vesicles, coat-contour occlusions, ambiguous focal adhesion/matrix overlap, and an ESCRT assembly that visually capped the open neck. Independent review also identified inward-facing Arf1 motifs on the new COPI bud; they were moved to the cytosolic outer membrane face. A native SVG geometry test verifies that their centers reach the exterior, that each motif touches the membrane, and that the prior inward-facing position is rejected.

Independent scientific and visual review passed all 28 drawings in all three treatments at 64, 256 and 512 pixels. The accepted record is `qa_artifacts/cell-architecture-family/20261013T165037Z/independent-artwork-review.json`; 81 originals were reused only after exact decoded-pixel comparison, and the three corrected COPI originals were inspected afresh. The corrected hemidesmosome reference attribution was also reviewed. This artwork acceptance does not substitute for native editor qualification or product-release approval, which remain separate.

The final whole-entity alias lists were independently accepted in `qa_artifacts/cell-architecture-family/20261013T165922Z/independent-artwork-review.json`. All 28 SVGs and 84 originals are unchanged; exact SVG, PNG-byte and decoded-RGBA comparisons preserve the artwork acceptance while binding the corrected current metadata.
