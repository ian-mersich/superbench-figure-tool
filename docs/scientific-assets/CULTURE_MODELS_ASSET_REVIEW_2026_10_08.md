# Culture Models & Functional Assays — artwork review

Twelve original editable SVGs extend the library with experimental arrangements
for culture, organoid manipulation and functional cell assays. These illustrations
show experimental setups; they do not represent measured outcomes.

| Asset | Distinction retained in the drawing |
| --- | --- |
| Perfused Cell–Matrix Culture Chip | Cell-containing matrix separated from connected adjacent media channels and open ports |
| Air–Liquid Interface Culture | Epithelial layer with air above and medium below a porous support |
| Hydrogel-embedded Organoid | External hydrogel, epithelial shell and enclosed lumen |
| Hanging-drop Spheroid Culture | Aggregate in a pendant drop beneath a support |
| Microwell Spheroid Aggregation | Cells collected at the bottom of a submerged rounded microwell |
| Epithelial Scratch Assay | Two epithelial sheets separated by an open scratch lane |

## Completed review

- Eleven primary precedents were selected, and their actual figure images were
  inspected before original geometry was drawn. Scientific citations, design
  decisions and limits are recorded in `culture-models-assays-science.json`.
- An independent reviewer inspected every one of the 36 individual original
  boards: twelve assets in Soft color, Cut paper and Technical ink, each at
  256 px and native 64 px on light and dark backgrounds. All passed.
- Scientific names, descriptions and limits for all twelve passed independent
  review. The submerged support was corrected to use actual openings rather than
  dark dashes on a filled plate, then freshly rendered and reviewed.
- `assay-visual-review.json` and `science-model-original-review.json` retain
  per-image verdicts. `final-original-review-binding.json` verifies unchanged
  SVG, transformed SVG, PNG and style hashes after acceptance metadata advanced.
- The canonical twelve-asset validator passed. Seven Python and two Node checks
  passed, including reproducibility, mutation and release guards, discovery and
  distinct experimental concepts. The registered eight-pack source library
  validates without collisions.
- The prepared source/web browser runner passed independent static review,
  compilation and six unchanged payload-classifier controls. This is preparation
  for actual browser qualification, not an in-app acceptance result.
