# Microbial drawings — 13 October 2026

The Microbial Structures & Interactions family contains 24 original editable
drawings across 19 concepts: twelve revised drawings and twelve additions.
Views and tail states stay with their biological parent; palette treatments do
not count as additions. Paired and chained cocci share one arrangement entry.

The additions include an encapsulated rod, a biofilm plan view, an unbranched
septate hypha, a contracted free phage, an isolated bacterial outer-membrane
vesicle, an endospore within its mother cell, paired and chained cocci,
pseudohyphae, an emerging germ tube, and short and long noncontractile phages.
The previously accepted Core rod bacterium and budding-yeast drawings remain
separate, unchanged primitives.

All twelve existing drawings retain their stable identifiers, biological part
identifiers, paint channels and default colors. Envelope layers have clearer
separation; capsules have smoother contours; phage head and tail detail is
less crowded; the open hyphal septal pores and viral fusion pore remain visible.
An independent review rejected an overly open septal partition and viral contents
touching a membrane branch. The revised septa and centered pore contents passed
fresh review in all three treatments.

## Published figures inspected before final drawing

Published figures supply biological relationships and morphology, never source
geometry. Actual primary image pixels were inspected at their original size.
Internal copies remain under the ignored `qa_artifacts/family-library/references/`;
they are not distributed as Library artwork.

| Source and figure | Drawing decision and limit |
