# Scientific asset visual review — 27 September 2026

An independent reviewer inspected contact sheets for all 291 assets in the library before this slice, then compared the revised drawings and three insertion styles at library thumbnail size. The goal was to judge whether a scientist could recognize the depicted structure or process without relying entirely on the asset name. The current library contains 294 vector styles across 267 concepts.

The Schwann cell/myelin drawing, pericyte, and cryostat were revised and accepted in the final focused review. The Schwann symbol now shows concentric myelin around a distinct axon, with the Schwann nucleus at the periphery. The pericyte now follows the outer surface of a capillary. The cryostat has a visible specimen block and sectioning blade. The newly added automated liquid handler reads clearly at thumbnail size; the confocal microscope reads as a microscope, although its exact confocal identity still depends on the label. Regulatory and exhausted T-cell drawings were subsequently revised to share neutral lymphocyte morphology. Their schematic context marks remain distinct, but neither silhouette is presented as a diagnostic cell-state feature; the library descriptions state the interpretation limit.

Cut paper is a useful alternate visual treatment for the entire library: flatter color, lighter contour, and a different visual weight from Soft color. Technical ink is a restrained line and palette treatment. Both retain the same underlying scientific geometry as the source SVGs. A future family with different silhouettes or anatomical detail should be authored and reviewed as separate vector drawings, not described as a result of these rendering controls.

## Follow-up review — 7 October 2026

The microglia, endothelium, tumor-associated antigen, neoantigen peptide and hypoxic-region revisions passed independent review at 64 px and 352 px in all three insertion treatments. Microglia now have asymmetric branching processes. The endothelial symbol shows a flattened lining cell beside a lumen and neighboring cells. The hypoxic-region symbol retains intact cells with vessel-distance and low-oxygen context. The antigen symbols use explicit cellular or MHC-presentation context; their descriptions state that shape alone does not establish tumor association, mutation origin or immunogenicity.

The first Cut paper review rejected faded Lumen, Low O₂, TAA and MHC labels. Moving those labels outside the softened contour group restored contrast; the outline color region controls both text fill and structural strokes. All 30 final originals passed the fresh independent review. The corrected drawings also passed the complete shared-editor/web, Light/Dark browser matrix. The findings are retained here and in the RNA-method review below, which records the temporary captures' disposition.
