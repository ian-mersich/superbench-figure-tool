# Microbial Structures & Interactions

Twelve original editable drawings add bacterial envelopes, capsules, spores and biofilms, a septate fungal hypha, and selected phage and membrane interactions. Biological components can be recolored separately. Soft color, Cut paper and Technical ink retain the same vector geometry.

These are illustrative schematics. They do not represent measured layer thicknesses, cell or protein counts, microbial resistance, or infection outcomes. Species-specific examples inform the depicted arrangement without making each drawing a universal mechanism.
