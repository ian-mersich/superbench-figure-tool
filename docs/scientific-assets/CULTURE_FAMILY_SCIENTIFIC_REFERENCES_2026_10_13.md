# Culture models and functional assays: drawing references

The expanded set depicts culture arrangements and illustrative assay geometry. It
does not show experimental observations, response, disease, cell viability or
quantitative readouts. Twelve existing drawings retain their identifiers and
editable biological parts; twelve additions provide six alternative forms and
six additional culture or assay concepts.

The preparation compared published figure panels within each biological family.
All eleven figures recorded in the earlier culture science record were inspected
again before authoring. Additional primary figures below were also inspected at
their available original resolution. Their geometry, marks, measurements and
layouts are not reusable components of the new drawings.

| [Williamson et al., 2018, Fig. 1A/G](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcmgh.2018.05.004) | Epithelium encloses a lumen within an external hydrogel; a microinjection needle crosses the shell and ends in the lumen. | No tissue identity, cargo identity, injected amount, targeting efficiency or measured fluorescence. |
| [Sato et al., 2009, Fig. 1a/c/e](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature07935) | A budded epithelial organoid has a central lumen continuous with epithelial buds, rather than several unrelated solid cells. | The generic budded form omits lineage markers, Paneth cells, fixed bud counts, time points and stem-cell claims. |
| [Richards et al., 2019, Fig. 1B/D and 2A/D](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2019.01.028) | An epithelial organoid and morphologically distinct stromal cells share a surrounding culture matrix. | Co-culture does not establish a stromal activation state, prostate identity, branching response or a requirement for stroma. |
| [Vinci et al., 2015, Fig. 1 and 2A](https://doi.org/10.3791/52686) | A compact spheroid can be held in a non-adherent medium-filled well, embedded in matrix, or accompanied by illustrative outgrowth into matrix. | The drawings are separate arrangements, not a time course or a treatment comparison. No tumor identity, invasion distance, rate or growth response is encoded. |

A solid spheroid has no epithelial lumen in these generic drawings. It therefore
differs from the hollow organoid in more than its color. The suspension and
hydrogel forms differ in their culture environment. Matrix outgrowth is explicitly
illustrative and must not be counted or interpreted as measured invasion.

| [Yamamoto et al., 2000, Fig. 1a-c](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-2567.2000.00973.x) and [Baxter et al., 2008, Fig. 1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12013-008-9013-8) | A detached collagen gel has a free-fluid annulus; an attached gel remains constrained by the well boundary. Cells are within the matrix. | The floating and attached forms are different mechanical arrangements. Size and morphology do not quantify contraction, force, stiffness, treatment effect or a mast-cell mechanism. |

The attached gel contacts the well boundary around its perimeter in the plan
view. Medium above or below that gel is occluded; an absent fluid annulus does
not indicate a dry culture.

## Epithelial interfaces and tissue slices

| Primary figure | Biological arrangement used | Limits retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lee et al., 2020, Fig. 1A](https://doi.org/10.1007/7651_2019_269) | Air is above an epithelial row on a porous insert; basal medium is below. | Ciliary marks indicate an illustrative ciliated culture, with no donor identity, epithelial proportions, resistance or ciliary beating claim. |
| [Misra et al., 2019, Fig. 1 culture inset](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-38603-w) | Medium covers a tissue slice on a porous support in the submerged form. | No pancreatic or tumor identity, exact thickness, oxygen tension or culture lifetime. |
| [Croft et al., 2018, Fig. 1G/K and interface-culture Methods](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.14500.2) | The inspected placement photographs and primary method describe slices on a membrane insert at the atmosphere-medium interface. The original cross-section uses that relation rather than copying a source schematic. | The interface form has basal medium reaching the porous support and an air-facing tissue surface. It does not assert a brain region, Alzheimer disease, tissue viability or that all slice cultures use this arrangement. |

These are live culture arrangements, distinct from a fixed histological section.
The support must contain real openings through which medium can reach its lower
surface; painted dashes on an uninterrupted solid plate would be misleading.
