## Cells and epithelial views

- [Margadant et al., PLOS ONE (2013), Figure 1C/H](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0065341), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0065341`: phase-contrast and actin images distinguish rounded cells from irregular spread adherent cells. Use an original rounded or spread outline, membrane, cytoplasm and nucleus. Cell spread is a morphology, not a universal healthy state or quantitative adhesion result.
- [Martins et al., PLOS ONE (2009), Figure 1A–C](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0007429), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0007429`: chick somitogenesis shows cuboidal and elongated epithelial cells with polarity and changing nucleus shape/position. Keep apical, lateral and basal faces coherent. Columnar is taller than wide; cuboidal is approximately equal in height and width; an apical view is a surface view. Do not add cilia, microvilli or a fixed nucleus position as a property of every epithelial cell.
- [Henderson et al., PLOS ONE (2007), Figure 2A–F](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0000749), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0000749`: whole-cell slices and segmented views provide an adjacent precedent for exposing internal compartments in a cutaway. This is the alga Ostreococcus tauri. Only the view/compartment distinction transfers: no chloroplast, organelle count, algal placement or specific dimensions belong in a generic mammalian-cell drawing. A cutaway should read as an explanatory view, not membrane rupture or cell lysis.

Rounded, spread and cutaway generic cells share one cell concept. Columnar, apical and cuboidal epithelial drawings are explicitly labelled views/morphologies, not claims of lineage identity.

## Neurons

- [Costa et al., eLife, Figures 1A/C/I and 6A/B](https://elifesciences.org/articles/104069), DOI `10.7554/eLife.104069` (eLife 2024;13:RP104069; institutional article PDF): primary images and schematics distinguish bipolar, transitional and pseudounipolar dorsal-root-ganglion neurons. A bipolar outline has two major processes attached directly to its soma. A pseudounipolar outline has one stem axon from the soma to a T-junction; both continuing branches are axonal, and the soma sits outside the through-path. Do not place the soma directly between the two T-junction branches or call either branch a dendrite.
- [Cangiano et al., PLOS ONE (2007), Figure 1B](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0001327), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0001327`: an individually labelled retinal rod bipolar cell provides an adjacent two-pole outline precedent. Its electrophysiology and retinal lamination are outside the generic icon's meaning.

Branching, bipolar and pseudounipolar drawings must be recognizable by connected geometry at small size. Do not encode electrical filtering, regeneration, transport rates, a particular neuronal subtype or measured axon caliber. Use a generic “bipolar morphology” label unless a retinal cell identity is intentionally supported.

## Nucleus, mitochondria, Golgi and ER

- [von Appen et al., Nature (2015), Figure 1a](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature15381), DOI `10.1038/nature15381`: the nuclear pore spans the envelope where inner and outer membranes join around an opening. Show two membranes separated by perinuclear lumen, continuous membrane curves around the pore, and the pore complex through the opening. A whole round/oval nucleus can show nucleoplasm and a membrane-less nucleolus. Do not add a membrane to the nucleolus or imply fixed pore counts, dimensions or atomistic composition.
- [Feric et al., Cell (2016), abstract/results text only](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5127388/), DOI `10.1016/j.cell.2016.04.047`: the nucleolus is a membrane-less nuclear compartment. Figure pixels were not examined. This supports omitting an enclosing membrane; no phase-separation or material-property claim belongs in the generic icon.
- [Henderson et al., PLOS ONE (2007), Figure 9A–D](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0000749), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0000749`: tomography shows outer membrane, inner boundary membrane and cristae connected to the inner membrane through narrow junctions. Keep inner folds attached; avoid isolated decorative squiggles or a universal full-width accordion.
- [Nikolaisen et al., PLOS ONE (2014), Figure 4A](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0101365), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0101365`: HUVEC mitochondrial images show irregular tubes and branching networks. A network drawing needs a continuous tubular boundary at junctions; a single/tubular form needs its double-membrane context. No morphology alone establishes energy output, health or disease. Do not reproduce measured branching, intensity, counts or scale bars.
- [Ladinsky et al., JCB (1999), Figure 9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2150572/), DOI `10.1083/jcb.144.6.1135`: flattened Golgi cisternae form stacks, with lateral bridges between equivalent levels of neighboring stacks. The oblique form changes view. A ribbon form needs neighboring stacks with coherent lateral connections, not simply a wider single stack. Keep lumen and inter-cisternal gaps readable; omit universal cisterna counts and traffic-direction arrows.
- [Nixon-Abell et al., Science (2016), Figures 3 and 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6528812/), DOI `10.1126/science.aaf3928`: peripheral ER has connected irregular tubular matrices alongside sheet structures. Rough-ER network and smooth ER should remain related membrane networks; rough ER has ribosome context on the cytoplasmic face. Smooth ER is a distinct biological entity, not a color treatment of rough ER. Do not claim all ER sheets are tubular or reproduce source density.

These references support symbolic compartment architecture. They do not establish the exact internal layout of an unspecified cell.

## Lysosome and autolysosome

- [Che et al., PLOS ONE (2019), Figure 3A](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0216553), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0216553`: TEM distinguishes autophagosomes from autolysosomes. A lysosome/cutaway has one limiting membrane and lumen; an autolysosome has one outer limiting membrane and heterogeneous material undergoing degradation, potentially with inner remnants. Two parallel enclosing membranes would read as an autophagosome. Generic contents must not imply a measured pH, flux, enzyme activity, cargo identity or degradation stage.

Lysosome cutaway is a form of lysosome. Autolysosome is a distinct concept.

## Antibody, receptor, enzyme, kinase and ligands

- [Maibom-Thomsen et al., PLOS ONE (2019), Figure 1a](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0217624), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0217624`: conventional IgG contains two heavy and two light chains; the heavy chains connect through the hinge and Fc. Each Fab comprises one light chain plus VH/CH1 heavy-chain fragment and one antigen-binding end. Fc comprises paired heavy-chain constant portions, without Fab tips. The familiar Y is a symbolic configuration, not a fixed natural geometry or an asserted functional state. Fab and Fc require distinct concepts.
- [Sun and Collier, PLOS ONE (2010), Figure 1A](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0010553), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0010553`: single-pass ANTXR2 supplies a topology precedent: extracellular domain, one uninterrupted membrane-spanning segment and cytoplasmic region. A generic receptor must not adopt ANTXR2-specific sequence, domain count or function. Bound/unliganded forms can retain one silhouette with a separate extracellular ligand added/removed; ligand absence does not establish inactivity. An isolated ectodomain omits membrane, transmembrane and cytoplasmic regions and is a distinct entity.
- [Kraut et al., PLOS Biology (2006), Figure 7A/B](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.0040099), DOI `10.1371/journal.pbio.0040099`: ligand–enzyme catalytic-site interactions inform an accessible pocket and fitted symbolic substrate. Avoid a sealed buried hole, universal lock-and-key precision, named atomic fold, reaction rate or activity claim.
- [Meharena et al., PLOS Biology (2016), Figure 1A/E/F](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.2000127), DOI `10.1371/journal.pbio.2000127`: kinase structure has two connected unequal lobes and an intervening cleft, with ATP and peptide substrate. Use symbolic lobes/cleft, not copied ribbons or residues. A static phosphate cue does not establish measured phosphorylation or reaction direction.
- The same kinase panel distinguishes a compact small molecule (ATP) from a peptide substrate. New generic ligands must distinguish a connected peptide chain from a compact abstract small-molecule symbol. Do not invent a named chemical structure, sequence, atom count, molecular size ratio or binding specificity.

## Protein assemblies

- [Chen et al., PLOS ONE (2014), Figure 2A/C/D](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0093156), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0093156`: TSP1 is a homotrimer of three identical-sequence chains. A generic homotrimer uses three matching subunit shapes/treatments in contact. Source colors identify separate copies; they do not make those copies different proteins.
- [Ren, PLOS ONE (2013), Figure 4a/b](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0077363), DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0077363`: hemoglobin provides a particular A2B2 assembly. Together with inspected IgG Figure 1a, this supports the subunit-count distinction only. A generic heterotetramer needs two matching A subunits and two matching B subunits, with shape/color contrast between A and B. Do not copy a hemoglobin fold, assert universal ring topology or mark a universal central active site.

The new homotrimer and A2B2 heterotetramer are distinct concepts. They must not duplicate the existing generic A2B heterotrimer or homo/heterodimers.

## Existing RNA methods

- [Macosko et al., Cell (2015), Figures 1 and 2](https://mccarrolllab.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/Macosko_Cell_1-s2.0-S0092867415005498-main.pdf), DOI `10.1016/j.cell.2015.05.002`: cell/bead compartment, oligo(dT) capture, shared bead barcode and distinct molecular identifiers. Preserve capture-bead and droplet-device meanings; do not guarantee one cell plus one bead in every droplet. Reverse transcription occurs after droplet breakage in Drop-seq; do not generalize that location to every droplet method.
- [Ståhl et al., Science (2016), Figures 1 and 2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27365449/), DOI `10.1126/science.aaf2403`: tissue on spatially barcoded capture features and distinct capture-probe roles. A feature does not guarantee a single cell. Avoid measured expression maps, intensities or platform-specific spacing.
- [Choi et al., Development (2018), Figure 1A–C](https://www.its.caltech.edu/~niles/refs/choi18.pdf), DOI `10.1242/dev.165753`: probe hybridization and tethered amplification are separate biological steps. HCR initiators/hairpins are DNA; do not label them RNA or transfer that amplification chemistry to generic RNA FISH.
- [Raj et al., Nature Methods (2008), methods/abstract only](https://www.nature.com/articles/nmeth.1253), DOI `10.1038/nmeth.1253`: multiple singly labelled probes support generic RNA-associated cellular puncta. Figure pixels were not examined. Generic in-situ puncta remain schematic, with no transcript counts, target identity, measured positions or scale.
- [Habib et al., Nature Methods (2017), methods only](https://wordpress.uchospitals.edu/basu-lab/files/2017/09/Habib_et_al-2017-Nature_Methods.pdf), DOI `10.1038/nmeth.4407`: isolated nuclei and nucleus/bead co-encapsulation support sample-context distinctions. No measured Figure 1 layout is used.

Five RNA-method figure panels were inspected (Macosko 1/2, Ståhl 1/2, Choi 1); the earlier total of seven also included the two ER figures. These four method drawings remain existing assets, not additional catalog concepts.
