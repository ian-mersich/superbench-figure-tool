# Cell structures and cell stress

These 20 concepts provide 40 original editable drawings. Each concept has two biological or contextual forms; the three Library drawing styles remain separate display treatments. The generator constructs coordinates from scratch. No published illustration, micrograph, molecular coordinate, sequence, numerical result or third-party vector is included.

The drawing source and primary references are in `scripts/cell_structure_stress_renderers.py` and `scripts/cell_structure_stress_science.json`. `scripts/seed-cell-structure-stress-assets.py` reproduces the original geometry. Its draft mode creates new prospective records rather than copying an earlier pack's rights grant. A separate rights decision and independent scientific, visual and native-editor review determine inclusion.

## Interpretation limits

- An isolated extracellular vesicle cannot establish exosome origin. Only the endosomal-release form depicts an endosomal-origin model; marker identity, purity, subtype and size are unspecified.
- Death-pathway drawings are schematic cues. Morphology alone cannot distinguish ferroptosis, pyroptosis, necroptosis or late lysis after another cause. A rounded detached cell alone is not evidence of anoikis.
- Anoikis depicts apoptosis associated with loss of appropriate matrix attachment. It is not a generic label for suspension culture or cell detachment.
- Nuclear foci are illustrative response-factor domains. They are not direct counts of breaks, mutations or repair events, and no specific marker is assigned to the generic icon.
- ER stress drawings are IRE1-informed shorthand, not complete PERK, IRE1 and ATF6 signaling diagrams. Sensor symbols do not establish activation or outcome.
- Arrows indicate vesicle release, cell detachment, mitochondrial approach or daughter separation as named in the form. They encode no rate, magnitude or measured direction.
- All drawings are scale-free. Protein symbols, branch counts, pore counts and organelle details carry no molecular stoichiometry or experimental measurement.

Three-style review also led to darker vesicle-coat and nascent-DNA colors so their distinction survives the alternate treatments. Independent review then identified optional inner nuclear contours crossing speckles or damage-response marks, caveolar/endosomal lumens painted as cytoplasm, and one mitochondrion touching its cell boundary. Those seven affected forms were corrected and all original captures repeated. Author self-review is separate from independent acceptance.
